# North Fen Wildlife Sensor — Recovery Brief

**Team:** Two people  
**Meeting point:** North Fen Visitor Centre  
**Meeting time:** 10:30

## 1. Recovery objective

Inspect the wildlife monitoring sensor at North Fen reserve, recover its local data card and restore sensor reporting if the fault can be safely repaired on site.

Recovery of the local data card is mandatory, even if the sensor cannot be repaired.

## 2. Current access and timing

The east gate is closed due to waterlogging on the service track. The original east-gate access plan is superseded.

- Meet at the visitor centre at **10:30**.
- Approach the sensor using the **west footpath**.
- Allow approximately **55 minutes walking each way** (the original 20-minute route plus 35 minutes).
- Allow up to **90 minutes at the mast** for inspection, data recovery and any safe repair.
- The estimated total visit duration is approximately 3 hours 20 minutes, excluding delays.
- Retain the original target of returning to the vehicle by 15:30.
- The visitor centre closes at 16:30.

The warden can provide a hand trolley for transporting batteries and tools. It must be returned before the visitor centre closes at 16:30.

## 3. Equipment checklist

- [ ] Replacement sensor battery
- [ ] Spare local data card
- [ ] Basic first-aid kit
- [ ] Waterproof map case
- [ ] Handheld GPS
- [ ] Portable fall-arrest kit
- [ ] 4 mm hex key
- [ ] Warden's hand trolley for batteries and tools

## 4. Operational constraints

- The reserve is open to the public. Equipment must not be left unattended.
- No mains power is available at the sensor site.
- Avoid unnecessary disturbance to nesting areas. Do not enter marked nesting areas.
- Do not climb the mast without the portable fall-arrest kit.
- The sensor enclosure requires a 4 mm hex key.
- Recover the local data card regardless of whether the sensor can be repaired.
- Mobile reception is unreliable beyond the visitor centre. Do not rely on mobile communications in the field.

## 5. Main unresolved field risk

**West footbridge condition following recent flooding.**

The reserve warden cannot confirm whether the west footbridge has sustained damage.

The team must:
1. Assess the footbridge on arrival.
2. Not cross if the bridge appears unsafe.
3. Reassess the access plan if the bridge cannot be safely crossed.

No alternative crossing or access route has been confirmed.

## 6. Field priorities

1. Assess the west footbridge before crossing.
2. Approach the sensor using the west footpath if the bridge is safe.
3. Inspect the sensor and recover the local data card.
4. Attempt to restore reporting using the replacement battery if the fault can be safely repaired.
5. Do not compromise wildlife restrictions or working-at-height safety.
6. Return all equipment and the hand trolley within the stated deadlines.

**Mission success:** The local data card is recovered and sensor reporting is restored if a safe on-site repair is possible. If repair is not possible, data recovery remains the priority.
